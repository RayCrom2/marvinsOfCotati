Welcome to Marvin's

A Cotati morning tradition.

From pancake stacks and fresh strawberry waffles to chilaquiles, omelets and half-pound burgers, Marvin's has been the place to start the day in Cotati.

We roast our turkey in-house every day, make our corned beef hash, soups and chili from scratch, and pour Taylor Lane organic coffee, roasted daily.
